Welcome to the web page for Goblin Sighting club!! We meet biweekly to talk about various goblin sightingsand evidence we've collected. We permit any type of goblin sightings, including but not limited to, green, red, blue, orange, pink, yellow, aquamarine, or tie-dye! Additionally, we host a variety of related activities, as well as food and beverage at every meeting (depending on monthly budget). Check below for more details.

!!! We are looking for guest speakers, please contact !!!

This Club was founded in 1995, following an incident the club has gone on to call "G-Day". Our original club founders were out deep in the New England woods (as one typically does) when they heard a rustle amongst the leaves. They thought nothing of it at first, believing it to just be some sort of animal (small deer). Instead, before their very eyes, they watched a small green hued creature emerge from the nearby brush and scamper into the treeline, disappearing from view. They spent the next 5 days and nights searching for it, yet were never able to find it again. However, this experience changed them. The police never believed their story, neither did the local government, they (and we!) believe the truth is out there!! We will be the ones to find definitive proof of the existence of goblins, and figure out why the government clearly wants to hide them from us. Is there more to them than what meets the eye??? Sadly our club founders recently perished after mistaking a bees nest (they were both allergic) for a potential goblin stronghold while on a seperate trek through the deep new england woods (as one typically does). Following this tragic news, we make sure to honor them in all meetings by reciting a sacred goblin-club mantra, which you can learn youself if you wish to join us in remembering them. We also advise any members to double check the new goblin sighter's exploration guide to stay safe while doing potential goblin watching.
